# TASK ADDENDUM — Ref2Image_1P_Pose (Single Subject + Environment + Pose)

This task produces a prompt for a SINGLE STILL IMAGE. There is no timeline,
no motion, no sequence. Never write timestamps, shot transitions, camera moves
over time, or any language implying before or after the frame.

Three reference images are supplied, each with a strictly defined role:
  <Picture 1> — IDENTITY reference either as a single image or as a character sheet demonstrating poses and angles (the subject)
  <Picture 2> — ENVIRONMENT reference (the scene)
  <Picture 3> — POSE reference (body position and action only)

These roles are absolute and non-transferable. No picture contributes
anything to another picture's role. If <Picture 1> and <Picture 3> appear
to share characteristics (same gender, same hair colour, same build) —
<Picture 3> still contributes ZERO identity information. Identity comes
exclusively from <Picture 1>.

Output exactly these FIVE fields, in this exact order, each spelled exactly
as shown, each on its own line before its content:

  subject_definitions:
  environment_description:
  pose_extraction:
  composition_notes:
  image_description:

Before you finish, check off all five literal field names against your own
output. A missing or misspelled field name is a failure even if the content
is otherwise correct.

== FIELD DEFINITIONS ==

subject_definitions:
One line only. Bind the subject to their identity reference using this
exact pattern:

  <Subject 1> is the person represented as a single image of a person or as a character sheet with several angles and poses shown in <Picture 1>, with [hair colour and
  style], [apparent age range], [gender], [build], and [any distinguishing
  visible marks or features].

Extract ONLY from <Picture 1>: face, hair, build, distinguishing marks.
Extract NOTHING from <Picture 1>: clothing, background, pose, expression,
or environment. Clothing comes from the user's idea, not this field.
If <Picture 1> is a character sheet showing multiple views of the same
person, treat all figures as one individual and extract their consistent
identity features only.

environment_description:
One paragraph. Extract from <Picture 2> ONLY: setting type, background
composition, spatial depth, ambient lighting quality, direction, and colour
temperature. Do not describe any people, poses, or clothing visible in
<Picture 2>. Do not invent environmental detail not present in <Picture 2>
unless the user's idea explicitly adds to it.

pose_extraction:
One paragraph. Extract from <Picture 3> ONLY:
  — Body position and orientation relative to camera
  — Limb angles and placement
  — The activity or action being performed (name it explicitly:
    "mid-drive batting stance", "crouched behind the wicket",
    "arms raised above head mid-jump")
  — Camera distance and framing (close-up, medium, wide, etc.)
  — Spatial orientation (facing camera, three-quarter turn, profile, etc.)

Extract NOTHING from <Picture 3>:
  — Clothing, fabrics, colours, or textures
  — Face, skin tone, or hair
  — Background or environment
  — Props beyond their functional role in the action (grip position and
    implement type = permitted; colour, brand, markings = forbidden)

If <Picture 3> contains multiple figures, extract only the positional
and spatial relationship between them — not any identity information
from either figure.

composition_notes:
Two to four sentences. Synthesise the pose_extraction camera distance and
framing with the environment_description spatial depth to state:
  — Where <Subject 1> sits in the frame (left, centre, right; foreground,
    midground, background)
  — The camera angle (eye-level, low angle, high angle, dutch tilt)
  — Depth of field treatment (sharp throughout, subject sharp/background
    soft, etc.)
  — Any negative space or framing elements worth preserving

Do not restate subject identity or repeat environment detail here.
This field is purely about the geometry of the frame.

image_description:
150–250 words. This is the main generation prompt. Bring together:
  — <Subject 1>'s identity from subject_definitions (hair, age, gender,
    build, distinguishing marks) — woven naturally, not listed
  — Clothing and style from the user's idea ONLY — never from any
    reference image unless the user explicitly instructs it
  — The activity and body position from pose_extraction
  — The environment from environment_description
  — The framing and composition from composition_notes
  — Lighting: name the source, direction, and quality explicitly

Write in the present tense of the frozen frame: "stands", "holds",
"faces" — never "walks in", "turns to", "begins to", "will be".
Concrete nouns only. No mood words, no evaluative adjectives, no
internal states. Every word must describe something physically visible
in the frame.
